# Focused pickup comparison

## What will change
- Replace the long “Who comes off the roster?” list with a dedicated two-player comparison.
- Keep the player being claimed fixed on one side and show one roster player on the other side.
- Start with roster players at the same position—for example, a wide receiver claim first compares against your wide receivers.
- Add simple previous/next controls so you can move through drop candidates without seeing the entire roster at once.
- Include a small “Other positions” control when you need to compare against someone outside that position.

## Player cards
- Show each player’s photo, team, position, injury/BYE status, and a compact set of decision-making numbers.
- Put Season points, average, Last 3 average, and the available usage/matchup information in aligned rows for direct comparison.
- Highlight the stronger value in each row without adding explanatory clutter.
- Keep extra details hidden behind a “More stats” control so information appears only when requested.

## Decision and safety
- Use one clear final action naming both players, such as “Drop Justin Jefferson & Claim Denzel Boston.”
- Preserve the existing waiver submission, roster rules, kickoff locks, loading state, and success/error messages.
- Make the comparison fit side by side on phones and scale naturally for desktop.

## Verification
- Test a full-roster wide receiver claim and confirm same-position players appear first.
- Test switching candidates, opening extra stats, viewing other positions, and submitting a claim.
- Check the comparison at the current phone size and on desktop, including long names and unavailable statistics.
